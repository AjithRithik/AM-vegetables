Privacy Policy

AM Vegetables (the "App") · Last updated: 6 October 2026

AM Vegetables is a fresh produce enquiry app. You browse vegetables, build a list, and send it to the shop on WhatsApp. This policy explains what information the App uses and what happens to it.

1. Information we handle

  • Contact and delivery details you type in: name, WhatsApp number, alternate phone, email (optional), address, pincode, landmark and delivery notes.
  • Location (optional): if you choose "Pick delivery location on map", the App can read your device's approximate or precise location to centre the map. Only the map point you confirm is used.
  • Your product list: the items and quantities in your cart.

2. How the information is used

  • To build the enquiry message that you send to the shop through WhatsApp, so the shop can confirm prices, availability and delivery.
  • If you pick a map location, a Google Maps link to that point is added to the message so the shop can find your delivery address.

Location is only accessed while the App is open and only after you grant permission. You can deny it and still use the App by dragging the map or typing your address.

3. Where your information goes

  • Stored on your device only: if "Save details for next time" is on, your details and chosen map point are saved locally on your phone so you don't have to re-type them. Turn the switch off, or clear the App's data, to remove them. We do not operate a server that stores your personal details.
  • Sent to the shop via WhatsApp: the message is sent only when you tap the send button, through WhatsApp, and is subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • Map tiles: the map picker downloads map images from OpenStreetMap servers, which can see your IP address and the map area requested. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • Catalogue: the App downloads the product list and shop information from our website. No personal data is sent in this request.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs in the App.
  • We do not take online payments in the App.
  • We do not collect your location in the background.

5. Data retention and your choices

  • Details saved on your device stay until you turn off saving, edit them, or uninstall the App / clear its data.
  • Messages you send via WhatsApp are held by the shop in its WhatsApp account. To ask for them to be deleted, contact the shop using the details below.
  • You can withdraw location permission at any time in your phone's Settings.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address.
